# 分镜设计最佳实践

> 来自 Awesome Seedance 的专业电影级分镜提示词模板

## 分镜提示词核心结构

```
【Style】风格定义, 视觉特征, 情绪氛围
【Duration】时长s
【Scene】场景描述

[时间轴] Shot N: 镜头类型
场景描述
动作描述
【Dialogue】对话/口型同步
```

## 专业术语参考

### 景别 (Shot Types)

| 缩写 | 全称 | 中文 | 用途 |
|-----|------|------|------|
| ECU | Extreme Close-up | 极端特写 | 强调情绪、细节 |
| CU | Close-up | 特写 | 角色面部、表情 |
| MS | Medium Shot | 中景 | 角色半身、动作 |
| MWS | Medium Wide Shot | 中全景 | 角色全身+环境 |
| WS | Wide Shot | 全景 | 展示环境、规模 |
| EWS | Extreme Wide Shot | 极端全景 | 史诗感、宏大场面 |

### 角度 (Angles)

| 角度 | 英文 | 效果 |
|-----|------|------|
| 俯视 | High Angle | 渺小感、弱势 |
| 仰视 | Low Angle | 压迫感、强大 |
| 平视 | Eye Level | 客观、中立 |
| 倾斜 | Dutch Angle | 紧张、混乱 |

### 运动 (Movements)

| 运动 | 英文 | 说明 |
|-----|------|------|
| 推 | Push In | 接近目标 |
| 拉 | Pull Out | 远离目标 |
| 摇 | Pan | 水平移动 |
|  tilt | 垂直移动 | Vertical Move |
|  跟 | Follow | 跟随主体 |
|  环视 | Orbit | 环绕主体 |

## 时间轴控制

### 标准格式

```markdown
[00:00-00:05]    前5秒
[00:05-00:10]    5-10秒
[00:10-00:15]    10-15秒
```

### 简写格式

```markdown
[00-05s]     前5秒
[05-10s]     5-10秒
[10-15s]     10-15秒
```

## 实际模板示例

### 1. 好莱坞赛车风格

```markdown
Style: Hollywood Professional Racing Movie (Le Mans Style), Cinematic Night, Rain, High Stakes Sport.
Duration: 15s.

[00-05s] Shot 1: The Veteran (Interior/Close-up).
Rain lashes the windshield of a high-tech race car on a track. The Veteran driver (in helmet) looks over, calm and focused. Dashboard lights reflect on his visor.
Dialogue Cue: He gives a subtle nod and mouths "Let's go."

[05-10s] Shot 2: The Challenger (Interior/Close-up).
Cut to the rival car next to him. The younger driver grips the wheel tight, breathing heavily. Eyes wide with adrenaline.
Dialogue Cue: He whispers "Focus" to himself.

[10-15s] Shot 3: The Green Light (Wide Action).
The starting lights turn Green. Both cars accelerate in perfect sync on the wet asphalt. Water sprays massively into the camera lens. Motion blur turns the stadium lights into long streaks of color.
```

### 2. 王家卫雨夜风格

```markdown
[Film Style]: 90s Hong Kong Art Cinema style, retro film feel, high ISO grain, ambiguous yellow-green tint, frame stepping effect, melancholic atmosphere.

[Core Dialogue]: "If memories were canned food, I hope they never expire."

[00:00-00:04] Shot 1: Through the Glass Peeping.
Scene: A rain-covered red public telephone booth.
Character: A man in a khaki trench coat holding the receiver tightly.
Emotional Performance: Through the glass refraction, see his eyes hollow yet deeply emotional. Rain flows down the glass, distorting his face like an oil painting.

[00:04-00:07] Shot 2: Extreme Close-up & Micro-expression.
Focus on the character's lips and half face. Lips tremble slightly.
Lighting: Street neon bokeh flows across his face, bright and dim alternately.

[00:07-00:10] Shot 3: Signature Slow-shutter Drag Shadow.
Character hangs up phone, turns around and walks into the rainy crowd.
Visual Effect: Frame stepping effect (stop-motion feel), motion blur, trailing shadows.
```

### 3. 国产短剧风格

```markdown
【Style】Popular Chinese web drama style (Mini-Drama Style), extreme fast-cut rhythm, high attractiveness filter, emotional outburst, romantic and heart-wrenching rainy night.
【Duration】15 seconds
【Characters】Deeply affectionate tycoon male lead (black coat, wet hair, red-rimmed eyes) VS stubbornly broken-hearted female lead (white dress, face full of tears).

[00:00-00:05] Shot 1: Rapid cut combination (Rapid Cuts).
Rainy street. Female lead decisively turns to leave (back view). Male lead rushes up and grabs her wrist (close-up). Female lead suddenly turns back, eyes showing pain filled with love and hate.
【Dialogue lip-sync】Female lead: "Let go! We're done!"

[00:05-00:10] Shot 2: Truth explosion (Intense Close-ups).
Male lead refuses to let go, rainwater streaming down both their faces. Male lead urgently pulls out a ring from his chest, raises it in front of her, fingers trembling.
【Dialogue lip-sync】Male lead: "Look carefully! I never deceived you!"

[00:10-00:15] Shot 3: Emotional dam burst (Climax).
The female lead sees the object, pupils shake (extreme close-up). Male lead pulls her into his arms and holds her tightly. Camera quickly rotates and circles around the two embracing.
【Dialogue lip-sync】Female lead: sobs silently
```

## 学习要点总结

1. **时间轴精确**: 使用 `[00:00-00:05]` 格式
2. **镜头语言专业**: ECU/CU/WS + 角度 + 运动
3. **场景要素完整**: 地点 + 时间 + 角色 + 动作 + 对白
4. **情绪标注**: 标注情绪关键词
5. **口型同步**: 使用 `Dialogue lip-sync` 标注
