# Summary

Tolkien Gateway is cited as an inspirational example of a comprehensive fan wiki—thousands of interlinked pages covering characters, places, events, and languages. The article suggests that an individual could personally build something similar while reading a book, with an LLM handling all the cross-referencing and maintenance.
