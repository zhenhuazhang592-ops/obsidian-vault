# Summary

NotebookLM is a Google LLM product that exemplifies the traditional approach to LLM + document interaction—uploading files for Q&A. It serves as a counter-example in the article, illustrating how most current systems work: retrieving relevant chunks at query time without accumulating knowledge persistently.
