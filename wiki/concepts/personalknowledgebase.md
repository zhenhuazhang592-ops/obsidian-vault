# Summary

A Personal Knowledge Base is a curated collection of information organized for individual use. The LLM Wiki article applies the LLM Wiki pattern specifically to personal knowledge management—tracking goals, health, psychology, self-improvement, reading notes, and building a structured picture of oneself over time.

## Applications

- Personal goal tracking
- Health and psychology monitoring
- Self-improvement journey
- Journal entries and article filing
- Reading notes and book companions
