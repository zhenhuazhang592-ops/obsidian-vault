# Summary

Knowledge Synthesis is the process of extracting key information from sources and integrating it into a coherent, interconnected wiki. It involves updating entity pages, revising topic summaries, noting contradictions, and strengthening or challenging existing synthesis as new sources are added.

## Key Activities

- Extract key information from new sources
- Update entity pages across the wiki
- Revise topic summaries
- Note contradictions with existing claims
- Strengthen or challenge evolving synthesis
- Maintain cross-references
