# Summary

Incremental Knowledge Building is the approach of gradually accumulating and integrating knowledge over time rather than starting fresh on each query. Each new source or query adds to the existing wiki, making it progressively richer with cross-references, contradictions noted, and synthesis evolving. The wiki compounds in value with every addition.

## Key Characteristics

- Knowledge compiled once, kept current
- Each source may touch 10-15 wiki pages
- Exploration results filed back as new pages
- Cross-references and synthesis already exist
- Value compounds over time

## Contrast

- Traditional: Re-derive knowledge on every query
- Incremental: Build upon accumulated synthesis
