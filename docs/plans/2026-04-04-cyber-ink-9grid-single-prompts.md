## 大图一：多角度9宫格

> 构图：单张大图，3×3=9个角度格，比例建议 1:1（正方形）

```
Generate a 3x3 grid image (9 panels) of a single female IP character
「赛博墨韵 Cyber Ink」, each panel showing a different viewing angle,
all panels must contain the same character with consistent appearance:
same face, same hairpin (matte black metal shaft with translucent deep
blue resin ink-drop head containing flowing golden fiber optics),
same cyan-blue ink-wash eyeliner, same black Taoist bun hairstyle.

Grid layout:
[1.Top-Left]     : Frontal full-body shot, character facing camera directly, centered
[2.Top-Center]    : Close-up portrait, face centered, eyes directly at viewer
[3.Top-Right]    : Left side profile, full body, showing elegant neck and bun silhouette
[4.Middle-Left]  : Right side profile, full body, showing sleeve embroidery detail
[5.Middle-Center]: 3/4 left view, full body, most dimensional dramatic angle (main pose)
[6.Middle-Right] : Back view, full body, showing bun back view and hairpin tail
[7.Bottom-Left]  : Top-down view, 30-degree tilt, looking down at head and shoulders
[8.Bottom-Center] : Low angle upward shot, 20-degree tilt, conveying power and authority
[9.Bottom-Right] : Rear 3/4 angle, 45-degree back view, dramatic cinematic perspective

Character appearance (repeat in every panel):
- Oval face, serene and profound eyes, ink-black pupils with subtle golden data streams
- Minimalist makeup, cyan-blue ink-wash eyeliner at eye corners, upward wing
- Black silky hair in modern high Taoist bun, a few loose strands at nape
- Data hairpin: matte black metal with translucent deep blue resin ink-drop, golden fiber optics flowing inside
- Modern New Chinese robe: loose black tech-velvet outer garment, silver-grey embroidered mountain patterns
- White high-neck base layer at collar
- Black obsidian and lapis lazuli mala bracelet (one bead is micro-holographic projector)
- Small asymmetrical silver earrings (left: circle, right: square)

Style per panel: Eastern classical aesthetics, cyberpunk lighting, ink wash painting,
high-fashion illustration, cinematic lighting. Dark moody background with subtle
blue and gold ambient light. 8k quality. Clean white grid dividers between panels.
```

**推荐比例**：1:1 正方形（最佳展示9宫格布局）


## 大图三：表情9宫格

> 构图：单张大图，3×3=9个表情格，比例建议 1:1

```
Generate a 3x3 grid image (9 panels) of a single female IP character
「赛博墨韵 Cyber Ink」, each panel showing a different facial expression,
all panels share the same character with consistent appearance:
same face, same hairpin, same cyan-blue ink-wash eyeliner, same black Taoist bun.
The expressions form a narrative emotional progression from calm to awakening.

Grid layout — 9 expressions in emotional progression:
[1.Top-Left]     : CALM — Neutral serene expression. Still water surface.
                   Mouth relaxed, gaze straight ahead with perfect tranquility.
                   Pupils: golden data streams flowing slowly.
[2.Top-Center]   : FOCUSED — Concentrated expression. Subtle frown between brows.
                   Eyes locked forward, pupils slightly dilated.
                   Pupils: golden data streams accelerating, converging at focal point.
[3.Top-Right]    : MEDITATING — Eyes half-lowered in meditation state.
                   Mouth corners relaxed. Utterly still inner gaze.
                   Pupils: golden data streams circulating slowly in circular pattern.
[4.Middle-Left]  : SHARP — Piercing sharp gaze like blades. Eyebrows slightly
                   raised at arch. Mouth a thin determined line.
                   Pupils: golden data streams bursting outward in radial pattern.
[5.Middle-Center]: SUBTLE SMILE — Zen master's knowing smile. Mouth corners raised
                   just 1-2 millimeters. Eyes full of quiet wisdom and warmth.
                   Pupils: golden data streams pulsing gently with warmth.
                   (THIS IS THE KEY HERO SHOT — center position)
[6.Middle-Right] : CONTEMPLATIVE — Gaze cast slightly downward. Lips in neutral
                   flat line. Fingers touching chin in deep thought.
                   Pupils: golden data streams spiraling downward in thinking pattern.
[7.Bottom-Left]  : AUTHORITATIVE — Chin slightly raised. Jaw set firm.
                   Eyes radiating absolute confidence and commanding presence.
                   Pupils: golden data streams condensed into sharp straight lines.
[8.Bottom-Center] : COMPASSIONATE — Brows relaxed. Eyes soft and kind, looking
                   gently downward. Mouth with gentle caring curve.
                   Pupils: golden light expanding outward like morning sunrise.
[9.Bottom-Right]  : AWAKENING — Eyes slightly opened wide in awe. Mouth parted.
                   Moment of satori. Eyes: golden data streams erupting and
                   surging outward in dramatic explosive pattern. Hairpin:
                   golden light pouring intensely from within. Holographic bead
                   now projecting a bright glowing mandala pattern.

Character appearance (repeat in every panel):
- Oval face, same facial features across all panels
- Cyan-blue ink-wash eyeliner at eye corners
- Black hair in modern high Taoist bun, loose strands at nape
- Data hairpin: matte black metal with translucent deep blue resin ink-drop,
  golden fiber optics (glows intensely in panel 9)
- Modern New Chinese robe, white collar visible
- Black obsidian and lapis lazuli mala bracelet

Style per panel: Eastern classical aesthetics, cyberpunk lighting, ink wash painting,
high-fashion portrait. Dark moody background with blue and gold ambient light.
Each panel slightly different lighting to reflect emotion. 8k quality.
Clean white grid dividers between panels.
```

**推荐比例**：1:1 正方形
**特别标注**：格5（中间位置）是整套的视觉锚点，优先确保这张质量
