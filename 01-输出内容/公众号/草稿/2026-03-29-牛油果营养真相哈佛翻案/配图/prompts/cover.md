# 被骂了10年的"热量炸弹"，哈佛刚刚给它翻了案 - Cover Image

## Concept
**Type**: Hero / Metaphor. Large focal avocado fruit, dramatically lit, positioned center-left with generous whitespace on the right. The avocado is depicted with a split cross-section revealing its rich, glowing green interior studded with a large seed — visually communicating "packed with nutrients." Floating around the avocado are data badges showing key vindication stats (Harvard shield, cardiovascular %, potassium mg). A subtle "VERDICT" or gavel element reinforces the scientific vindication theme. Clean, bold, instantly readable on mobile.
