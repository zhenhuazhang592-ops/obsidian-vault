# 辨别真假牛油果油 步骤图

## Layout
Horizontal step-by-step flowchart, 5 steps in sequence.

## STEPS
1. **看价格** - 价格 <40元/100ml 要警惕
   Icon: Price tag with question mark

2. **看产地** - 墨西哥/秘鲁更可靠
   Icon: Globe or map pin

3. **冷冻测试** - 冷冻后变浑浊=真
   Icon: Snowflake / freezer

4. **低温观察** - 有絮状物=正常
   Icon: Oil bottle in fridge

5. **选正规渠道** - 大超市/旗舰店更安全
   Icon: Shopping cart / store

## CONNECTIONS
Bold arrows connecting each step. Circular step numbers in coral color.
