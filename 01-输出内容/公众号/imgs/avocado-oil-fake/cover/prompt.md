# 牛油果油封面图

## Concept
A conceptual cover image that conveys the message "82% of avocado oil is fake" with visual impact.

## Main Visual
- Center: Giant bold text "82%" in coral red (#E07A5F)
- Below 82%: Smaller text "的牛油果油是假的" in dark brown
- Left side: Simple oil bottle icon with X mark (fake)
- Right side: Simple oil bottle icon with checkmark (real)
- Background: Clean white/cream with subtle geometric shapes

## Visual Elements
- Bold percentage number as focal point
- Two oil bottle icons showing contrast (fake vs real)
- Warning/exclamation feeling
- Simple, clean, modern

## Color Palette
- Background: White or cream (#F5F0E6)
- Main text 82%: Coral Red (#E07A5F)
- Secondary text: Dark brown (#3D3D3D)
- Accent elements: Mint Green (#81B29A)
- Decorative: Soft yellow (#F2CC8F)

## Typography
- Title text should be bold and prominent
- Use clean sans-serif style
- Numbers should be dominant

## Mood
Bold, impactful, slightly alarming but not scary. Conveys importance of the topic.

## Style
Flat vector illustration with warm hand-drawn accents. Clean modern look. White space dominant. Minimal decorative elements.

## Text (exact)
Must include these Chinese texts:
- "82%" (large, dominant)
- "的牛油果油是假的" (below 82%)

## Aspect Ratio
16:9 (1920x1080)

## Composition
Centered layout. 82% number takes up ~40% of visual space. Two bottles at bottom or sides. Clean and impactful.
